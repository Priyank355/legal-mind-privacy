Legal Mind · Data controls

Delete your account

Request deletion of your Legal Mind account and data associated with it.

Option 1 — Delete from the app

Open the installed Legal Mind app and go to Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account & associated data. The app will ask you to sign in with Google again to confirm your identity. The server-side request must complete before the app clears local practice records and app-private document copies.

This is the most direct route for accounts that can still sign in.

Option 2 — Submit a request if you cannot access the app

Send a request from the Google account email address used in Legal Mind. We may ask for proportionate information needed to verify that you control the account. Do not send your password, OTP, client names, case papers or other privileged material.

Email account deletion support

The link opens your email application and does not submit the request automatically. Keep a copy of your request. The operator must monitor this address, verify account ownership proportionately and complete or respond to the deletion request promptly. This static site does not itself execute server-side deletion; email requests require manual handling.

What is deleted

When the in-app server-side deletion operation is successfully completed, it removes the Firebase Authentication account, the user's Firebase records, chamber memberships/invitations and the owner's chamber and its nested records. Legacy experimental safety reports associated with the account are also targeted for deletion. Where schedule entries exist in another user's shared chamber, the account author identifier is anonymised.

If you own a chamber, deleting it removes schedules, invitations, member access and shared records for that chamber and affects other members. Export what you are lawfully entitled to retain and notify collaborators first.

What may remain elsewhere

Feedback or support emails you send from your mail app are not automatically removed from the sender's or recipient's mailbox; contact the relevant mailbox operator if you need to request their deletion. User-exported files, email or clipboard copies and backups under your control are not erased by the app. Local-only records on another offline device cannot be remotely erased by an account deletion request; clear Legal Mind storage on each device you control. Data may be retained only where required by law or for a legitimate security/compliance purpose.
