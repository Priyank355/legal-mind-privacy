Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Read this policy before entering personal or confidential legal material into Legal Mind.

1. Operator and contact

Legal Mind is an independent legal-practice productivity application operated by Advocate Priyank Singh. The operator identity should match the developer identity used in the app store listing. Privacy questions and data requests: advpriyanksingh355@gmail.com. The app is not an official court or government application and does not imply endorsement by any court or public authority.

2. Information handled

Depending on the features used, Legal Mind may process Google sign-in identifiers and profile information (such as name, email and profile image); profile and chamber settings; client, matter, hearing, calendar, note, document, task, invoice and payment records you enter; reminders and notification preferences; and feedback you choose to send. Most practice records in this release are stored locally on your device. Chamber schedules and account-deletion requests may be processed by Legal Mind Firebase services where those services are enabled and configured. The current release does not include an AI assistant or an external legal-research provider integration.

3. Sign-in, Firebase and diagnostics

Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication authenticate the account. Firebase Cloud Functions and Cloud Firestore support selected account, chamber-sharing and account-deletion features when those services are deployed and configured. Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash diagnostics such as app/device version, exception information and stack traces when crash reporting is enabled. The app is designed not to intentionally log client documents or case text in diagnostic messages; accidental contextual data cannot be ruled out in every failure condition.

4. Official court websites and external sources

The live display board and court-data tools retrieve publicly accessible information from official court websites. Those external services may receive ordinary network request information under their own policies. Official pages may publish delayed, incomplete or unavailable information. Legal Mind displays source notices and the time of the last successful snapshot so that saved data is not mistaken for live data. Verify court status and orders at the linked official source. Legal Mind does not claim court or government affiliation and does not bypass CAPTCHA or access controls.

5. Advertising

The source configuration may use Google Mobile Ads test identifiers. A production release may use Google Mobile Ads with production identifiers. The ads SDK may collect or receive device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and other data as described by Google's applicable SDK disclosures and consent/settings required for the audience and region. The app checks Google UMP consent before release ad requests and presents a privacy-options entry point when UMP requires one. The operator must configure the correct AdMob privacy messages and complete Play Console Data Safety answers against the final production SDKs and configuration.

6. Permissions and control

The reviewed Android manifest requests internet access, notification permission, vibration and boot-completed handling for app services and reminders. Notifications can be controlled in Android settings. No camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions are declared in the reviewed manifest. SDK behaviour and future app versions may change; this policy and the Play Console disclosures must be updated when actual data practices change.

7. Retention and deletion

Local practice records remain on the device until you remove them or complete account deletion in the app. The account-deletion flow is designed to remove the Legal Mind Firebase Authentication account, user-scoped Firebase records, chamber access/invitations and the owner's shared chamber records; schedule authorship in another user's chamber is anonymised. The owner-managed chamber and its nested schedules, invitations and member access are deleted because those records are tied to the owner's account. This can affect other chamber members; export records lawfully and notify them before deleting an owner account.

Feedback or support emails you send yourself may remain in your or the recipient's mailbox and are not automatically removed when the app account is deleted. User-exported files, screenshots, clipboard contents, email copies and backups under your control are outside the app's ability to erase. Local-only records on another offline device cannot be remotely erased by this account-deletion flow; clear Legal Mind storage on each device you control. Certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security/compliance reasons, with retention limited to that purpose.

8. Security

The app uses Firebase Authentication for account identity, server-side validation and access-controlled Firestore rules for shared chamber data. No software service can guarantee absolute security; keep your device protected and restrict shared-chamber permissions carefully.

9. Contact and updates

For privacy questions, account deletion or correction of an inaccurate disclosure, contact advpriyanksingh355@gmail.com. This policy must be updated when the app, SDKs, providers, retention practices or legal requirements change. The public page, in-app notice, release build and Play Console declarations must remain consistent.
