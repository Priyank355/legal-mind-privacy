Account deletion

In the installed app, open Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account & associated data. The app asks you to authenticate with Google again before starting server-side deletion. If you cannot access the app, use the external request page.

Deletion covers data controlled by Legal Mind as described in the Privacy Policy. It cannot erase material already exported, copies in email or clipboard history, or data independently held by third parties.

Data handled by Legal Mind

Depending on the features used, the app handles Google sign-in/profile information, local practice records, matter and hearing details, documents you import, chamber-sharing information, notifications and feedback you choose to send. The current release does not include an AI assistant or external legal-research provider integration.

Live court-board information is retrieved from official court websites. The source may be delayed or unavailable; the app identifies official-source notices and timestamps saved snapshots.
